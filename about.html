ABOUT GRADBRIDGE

Helping graduates bridge
education and employment.

GradBridge to Career is a student-focused toolkit that makes career preparation less confusing and more practical.

OUR PURPOSE

Confidence for the world of work.

Finishing a qualification is a big achievement, but the move into employment can bring new challenges. It can be difficult to prepare a CV, manage applications, find reliable resources and plan a first salary.

Our toolkit brings these tasks together in one accessible website. It is designed to support planning and learning; users should always check employer requirements and official information before applying.
